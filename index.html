NETRUNNER://CCNA

A free browser game and study aid for the Cisco CCNA 200-301 exam. It is a non-commercial cyberpunk fan-art project. Players meet characters who teach networking, then fix network faults in a simulated Cisco IOS console. Study cards use spaced repetition.

Sign in with Google (optional). Signing in lets a player keep their game record across devices. The game uses the player's name, email and picture only to show who is signed in, and saves the game record in the player's own Google Drive app folder. The game cannot see any other file in the player's Drive. There is no server and no data is shared.
