Privacy Policy

NETRUNNER://CCNA ("the game") is a free, non-commercial art project and study aid for people preparing for the Cisco CCNA exam. Last updated 28 September 2026.

1. The short version

The game has no server and no database. Your game record lives in your browser and, if you sign in with Google, in your own Google Drive. We do not sell, rent or share your data. We do not use ads or trackers.

2. What the game stores

  • Your game record: your handle, progress, study cards, settings and in-game items.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture, used only to show who is signed in and to find your record.

The game does not collect your location, contacts, payment details or any other data.

3. Where it is stored

  • If you sign in with Google: your game record is stored only in your Google Drive app folder. This is a hidden folder that only this game can read. The game cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. Your browser keeps only your name, email, picture and the name of your last handle, so it remembers you until you sign out. Signing out removes them.
  • If you play with a handle and passcode only: your game record is stored in your browser (local storage), on your device.

Google asks you to tick a box that lets the game keep its own data in your Drive. That box is how your save file is stored. Without it, signed-in progress cannot be saved.

4. Google user data

The game asks Google for these permissions: openid, email, profile and drive.appdata. Data from Google is used only to sign you in and to save and load your game record. It is never sent to anyone else and is not used for ads or to train AI models. The game's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Donations

If you choose to donate, the payment is handled by a third-party payment service under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your card or bank details. We may see the name, email and message you give that service, and use them only to say thank you.

7. Your choices

  • Press SIGN OUT in the game at any time.
  • Remove the game's access, and its app-folder data, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove the local copy.
  • Email us to ask any question about your data.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you use Google sign-in, you must meet Google's minimum age in your country.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
